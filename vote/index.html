Make a Plan to Vote

Voters who make a concrete plan are far more likely to actually cast a ballot. Take five minutes to make yours below, and then help three other people make theirs. Every resource here is free and nonpartisan.

Make Your Plan

  • Register or Check Your Registration ↗ The official U.S. government portal. Register or confirm you're registered for your state.
  • Early Voting Dates ↗ Look up your state's key dates and every way you can cast a ballot.
  • Find Your Polling Place ↗ Know exactly where to go before Election Day.
  • See What's on Your Ballot ↗ Research the candidates and measures so you're ready before you go.
  • Questions or Problems? Call 866-OUR-VOTE ↗ Free, nonpartisan Election Protection hotline (866-687-8683) for any voting issue.

Help Others Vote

  • Become a Poll Worker ↗ Polls can't open without workers. It's often paid, and it keeps democracy running.
  • Bring Someone With You Text three friends or family members: check their registration together, share the deadlines above, and offer a ride. A plan shared is a plan doubled.
